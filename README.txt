BUNNYBITCH - THUMBY COLOR
===========================

A cute and colorful platform game inspired by classic platformers.

CONTROLS
- Left/right: move
- A: jump
- B: jump before buying the blaster; shoot after buying it
- MENU: open the Flower Shop
- Shop: Up/Down selects, A buys and B closes

INSTALLING WITH THONNY
1. Connect the Thumby Color and turn it on.
2. Open Thonny and click the red STOP button.
3. Open the Games folder under Raspberry Pi Pico.
4. Upload the complete BunnyBitch folder to /Games.
5. Turn off the Thumby Color, disconnect the cable and turn it on again.

FOLDER STRUCTURE ON THE THUMBY
/Games/BunnyBitch/main.py

This version includes:
- A title screen
- Running and jumping
- Platforms and gaps
- Nine collectible flowers
- A finish flag and victory screen
- Four different enemy types: snail, caterpillar, mushroom and spiky monster
- Three lives and a game-over screen
- Jumping on enemies to defeat them
- Three levels in World 1
- Horizontal and vertical moving platforms
- Increasing difficulty in each level
- A boss battle against King Slime
- A boss health bar and five-hit stomp battle
- World 2: Magic Mushroom Forest
- Three magical forest levels with glowing mushrooms
- New beetle, ghost and bat enemies
- A seven-hit boss battle against Queen Morella
- World 3: Snow and Ice World
- Three snowy levels with icy moving platforms
- New penguin, snowball and ice-bug enemies
- An eight-hit boss battle against Frostfang
- World 4: Candy World
- Three colorful candy levels with lollipops and pink hills
- New gummy, cupcake and lollipop enemies
- A nine-hit boss battle against the Candy Dragon
- World 5: Volcano World
- Three difficult lava levels with fast moving platforms
- New ember, fireball and salamander enemies
- A ten-hit final boss battle against the Volcano Titan
- World 6: Night Dream World
- Three moonlit dream levels with stars and cloud platforms
- New starling, owl and shadow enemies
- A twelve-hit final boss battle against Nightmare Moon
- A special ending after completing all six worlds
- Flower Shop menu with flowers used as currency
- Flower Blaster power-up for 8 flowers
- Super Jump power-up for 10 flowers, active for about 12 seconds
- Extra Life power-up for 15 flowers, up to five lives
- Blaster, Super Jump and Extra Life pickups also appear inside normal levels
- Super Jump can be bought or found again after its timer expires
- The Blaster is lost whenever the player loses a life or is hit by an enemy
- The shop displays the remaining Super Jump time
- The inventory resets only after Game Over or starting a completely new run
- Duplicate Blaster or Super Jump pickups award three flowers instead
