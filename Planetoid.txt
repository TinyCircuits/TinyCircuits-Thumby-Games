<Planetoid> 
------------------------------------------------------

<Controls>
Menu screen has Play, Gravity, Decay, Super and use the D pad to control the menu

1. Menu tutorial) 
Click Play to start the game, Gravity is you can turn it off or on, Decay is the longer you stay on a planet the smaller it becomes, Super is when you click "play" you spawn on top a giant planet

2. Instructions) 
D pad = move around

Up arrow on D pad and A/B is jump

Don't get crushed by other planets or fall off the screen. That's how you die

The longer you stay off ground the faster the points go 

6. GUI)
Its just the score

7. Gameover) 
When you die it shows your score and your Best score and if clicked on A you will retry but if clicked on B that menu which sends you to menu
