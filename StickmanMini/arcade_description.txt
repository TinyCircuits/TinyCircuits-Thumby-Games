Stickman Mini
Version 1.0
Made by: TU APODO AQUI

A platformer adventure across 8 worlds: Jungle, Snow, Volcano, Water City,
Cloudland, Wonder Beach, Outer Space and Blacky's Castle!
Collect gems, beat the clock and defeat 2 bosses: King Shark and Blacky.

Controls:
  Left/Right = walk
  A = jump (in water: tap A to swim)
  B = punch
  B while running = belly dive attack!

Una aventura de plataformas en 8 mundos. Junta gemas, gana antes de que
se acabe el tiempo y vence al Rey Tiburon y a Blacky!
