BunnyBitch is a colorful platform adventure made for Thumby Color.

Guide a cute but fearless bunny through six different worlds: Sunny Meadow, Magic Mushroom Forest, Snow and Ice World, Candy World, Volcano World, and Night Dream World. Collect flowers, defeat different enemies, ride moving platforms, and battle a boss at the end of every world.

Open the Flower Shop to spend collected flowers on power-ups. Buy or find the Flower Blaster, temporary Super Jump, and Extra Lives.

Controls
- Left/Right: Move
- A: Jump
- B: Jump before obtaining the blaster; shoot while carrying the blaster
- Menu: Open the Flower Shop
- Shop Up/Down: Select an item
- Shop A: Buy
- Shop B or Menu: Close the shop

Platform: Thumby Color
