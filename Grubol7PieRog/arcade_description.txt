Grubol 7 PieRog

One of the most addictive puzzle experiences on Thumby: match symbols across a 4x4 grid and clear as many tiles as you can before you run out of space.

Place 2- and 3-tile pieces to link identical symbols, earn wonga, and level up. Use combos, jokers, and quirky dwarfs - or jump straight into Time Attack mode.

U/D/L/R move / switch floors
A place piece (hold for dwarf market)
B rotate
