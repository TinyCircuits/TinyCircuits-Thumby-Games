BlackThumb is a variation of blackjack made to be played on the Thumby including plenty of diverse features:
Classic Blackjack gameplay (hit / stand / double down)
Bet system with multiple stake options (including ALL-IN mode)
Streak-based multiplier system for bigger winnings
High score saving (persistent storage)
Simple 2-row card layout (fits up to 10 cards)
Dealer hidden-card mechanic
Sound effects + win/lose tunes
Stats screen (money, best score, streak, multiplier)
Tips/help screen with controls guide
Lightweight pixel-style UI optimised for Thumby screen
Randomized gameplay each round (true seed randomness)
Quick restart / one-button interaction flow
