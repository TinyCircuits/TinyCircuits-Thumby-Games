=========================================
TINY SKYLINES - MAYOR'S MANUAL

Welcome to Tiny Skylines! Your goal is to build a thriving, bustling metropolis right on your Thumby. Manage your budget, provide jobs and power, and keep your citizens happy to watch your city grow!

CONTROLS

D-PAD: Move the targeting reticle around the map. Pushing against the edge of the screen moves the camera.
B BUTTON: Cycle through your Build Menu. Watch the bottom of the screen to see what structure is currently selected and its cost.
A BUTTON: Build the selected structure on an empty tile.
A BUTTON (on a building): Demolish the structure. You will be refunded 25% of its original cost.
HOLD A + B: Restart the game and clear the city (hold until the bottom progress bar fills up).

THE HUD (Heads Up Display)

At the bottom of your screen is your city's dashboard:

FACE ICON: Overall Happiness. Ranges from ecstatic to sad. Keep them smiling to attract more residents and prevent crime!
COIN : Your Treasury ($). Used to build structures.
PERSON : Your Population. Your high score is based on how high you can get this number.
LIGHTNING : Power Balance. Represents your Energy Supply minus your Energy Demand. Keep this number Positive (+)!

BUILDINGS & ZONING

HOUSE ($10): Provides housing for your citizens. Houses need nearby power and jobs to thrive. If a house is very happy, it will automatically upgrade to a High-Density Apartment!
STORE ($15): The backbone of your economy. Stores provide jobs and generate tax income. Place them near homes and roads to maximize their profits. They can also upgrade to High-Density Commercial towers!
PYLON ($20): Generates +6 Power for your city. Absolutely crucial for growth, but beware: every Pylon costs a small upkeep fee every simulation tick.
TREE/PARK ($8): Boosts the happiness of all immediately surrounding buildings. Great for triggering House upgrades!
ROAD ($2): Connects your city. Placing buildings next to roads boosts commercial income and residential happiness.

CITY SIMULATION & RULES

GROWTH: Your population will only move in if you have empty Houses, a positive Power Balance, and decent Happiness.
INCOME: You earn money based on your Commercial buildings' success and taxes from your population.
UPKEEP: Having too many Pylons or High-Density buildings will drain your treasury. Don't build too fast!
PENALTIES: Your city's Happiness will plummet if you have high Unemployment (not enough Stores), a negative Power Balance, or if you fall into Debt (negative money).

RANDOM EVENTS

Keep an eye out for the "TINY TIMES" newspaper popping up!

FESTIVALS & ELECTIONS: High happiness can trigger positive events that give you massive cash bonuses or happiness boosts.
RECESSION & EARTHQUAKES: Bad luck can strike at any time, draining your treasury or destroying random buildings.
ARSON: If you let your city's Happiness fall too low, angry citizens might burn down their own neighborhoods!

Good luck, Mayor!
