SRAOSHA VANISH
Version: 0.11.1
Created by AZ-aeon
A monochrome arcade puzzle set in the original world of AEON.
The angels always win! Your challenge is to choose the best attack materials to match the weaknesses of mysterious, malformed gods.
Choose 3 ammunition types from 5 candidates and aim for a high score before time runs out.
FEATURES
- 30 unique enemies
- 22 ammunition types
- 11 angels with 20 different forms
- 63 encyclopedia entries
- 1, 3, 5 and 10-minute modes
- Music, sound effects and saved high scores
CONTROLS
Title: A to start, LEFT/RIGHT/UP for encyclopedias.
In-game: A to continue or lock an ammo choice.
Ammo selection: LEFT/RIGHT to move, B to unlock.
Encyclopedias: Direction buttons to browse, B to return.
Hold B for 10 seconds during gameplay to return to title.
Victory belongs to the angels. The score is yours!
